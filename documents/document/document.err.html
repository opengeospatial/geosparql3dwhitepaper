<p id="_d8df9043-17eb-c2ee-86bc-e9bd4c8595a9">The related work above primarily address 3D geometry semantics. A complementary aspect is the computational processing of the 3D representations. <title id="_8accff95-3327-b0bc-3ad2-74f3b5a8ed2b">OpenCASCADE</title>
<p id="_4ebc0ddc-54f1-5a21-77fb-853d7fcd7231">OpenCASCADE is an open-source software platform for 3D geometric modelling and CAD applications. It provides data structures and algorithms for representing and processing geometric and topological entities, including points, curves, surfaces, solids, and B-Rep models. The platform supports operations such as geometric transformations, Boolean operations, intersections, and shape analysis. Its explicit representation of geometry and topology makes OpenCASCADE relevant for the computational processing of 3D solids and for investigating how B-Rep-based geometry could be supported by a GeoSPARQL 3D implementation.</p>
