<title id="_5238d667-cd98-939b-b204-48e18fd0f669">OpenCASCADE</title>
<p id="_ac8001a0-8bff-dce8-df6e-39a2ce3e32d8">OpenCASCADE is an open-source software platform for 3D geometric modelling and CAD applications.
